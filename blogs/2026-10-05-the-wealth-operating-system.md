Modern wealth and equity management hinges on a massive, hidden challenge: turning unstructured documents, like fund statements and complex financing agreements, into actionable records. Mantle’s Wealth Operating System reimagines this entire pipeline by uniting collection, extraction, personalization, and automated workflows into a single source of truth. But as financial technology takes on more of the heavy lifting, one question becomes decisive: how can software interpret complex financial context without sacrificing accuracy?
