Switching cap table platforms sounds scarier than it is. Your cap table is one of the most sensitive documents in your company, so it’s reasonable to be careful. But “careful” doesn’t mean “hard.” It means knowing what to check, who to notify, and what not to cancel until you’re sure it’s the right time.
