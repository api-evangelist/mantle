Mantle has partnered with Dream Payments to bring payment coordination into Mantle Portal. Capital calls, approvals, and wire execution have lived in three disconnected systems: a document platform, an email inbox, and a bank portal. With Dream Payments' infrastructure built into Mantle, allocators can move from decision to execution without ever leaving the platform that tracks the investment in the first place.
